**Jiayang Wang, Julia A. Palacios, and Claudia Solís-Lemus**

We introduce a triangular matrix representation of rooted, ranked, unlabeled phylogenetic networks and define distance metrics using matrix norms. These methods support comparisons of networks with different numbers of hybridization events and with either isochronous or heterochronous sampling.
